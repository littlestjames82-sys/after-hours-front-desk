☎️ After-Hours Front Desk

A voice receptionist that lives on the shop's own computer — no cloud, no per-minute fees, in the shop's own voice profile.

Simulator demo. The caller side is typed (a stand-in for the phone line — real phone wiring is the paid next step). The front desk's replies are generated live by a local open-weight model and spoken by the Sopro voice engine, both running on this machine. The shop below is fictional.

Press Call — the front desk answers out loud. Then type as the caller.

Built by Ghost Developer Studio · Brain: Gemma (open weights) via llama.cpp · Voice: Sopro V2 Turbo (Apache-2.0) · Nothing leaves this machine.
